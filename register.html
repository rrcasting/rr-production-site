Visa & residence card

Why we ask for your residence card Paid work in Japan requires us to confirm your status. This applies to every performer, no exceptions. We check the card, record only your visa type and expiry date, and then delete the image. Fill the frame with the card and shoot it straight from above, so the number and the text on the back can be read.
A tourist / temporary visitor status cannot be registered — all our projects are paid work.

Photos are uploaded as soon as you choose them and are used only for your registration (identity and work-permission check, casting profile). If you do not complete registration, they are deleted automatically within 24 hours.

Japanese nationals do not have a residence card. Please send the photo page of your Japanese passport instead. We check it and delete the image, the same as with residence cards. If you do not have a passport, message us on WhatsApp.
Send the FRONT only. Do not send the back — it shows your My Number.
If you hold a Student or Dependent visa, the 28-hour weekly limit counts all of your work together. Staying within it is your responsibility — check that a shooting day will not take you over.

Photos (face and full body — two separate photos)

A phone photo is fine. What matters is that the production can see your face and your build clearly.

  • FACE — from the shoulders up, looking straight at the camera. No sunglasses, no hat, no mask, no filter. Taken within the last 6 months.
  • FULL BODY — head to feet, standing straight, plain background, clothes that show your build (not baggy).
  • EXTRA (optional, up to 3) — another angle, a smile, or a photo from a shoot. These help you get selected.
